Guilty Minds Lab

Markus Kneer’s research group at the IDea_Lab, University of Graz. He founded the lab at the University of Zurich in 2019.

The research

The lab works on foundational questions raised by artificial intelligence, combining philosophical analysis with empirical methods from the cognitive sciences: experiments, statistical analysis and the analysis of language. Among its questions are who is responsible when an AI system causes harm, how AI systems can be aligned with human values, what people expect of the artificial agents they interact with, how algorithmic bias should be addressed, and when AI can be trusted.

The lab takes its name from its first project, on how people ascribe guilty minds, or mens rea: intention, knowledge, recklessness and negligence. That work compared lay people with professional judges and legal experts across several legal systems, and now extends to how people judge artificial agents and AI systems used in legal decisions.

Funding

The lab has secured several million in competitive funding to date, including from the Swiss National Science Foundation, HERA/CHANSE and Innosuisse.

The team

The lab is led by Markus Kneer and brings together philosophers, cognitive scientists and computer scientists. Its current members are listed on the IDea_Lab’s page for Ethics of Artificial Intelligence.
