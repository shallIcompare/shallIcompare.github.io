Guilty Minds Lab

Experimental research on moral and legal judgement, led by Markus Kneer. The lab’s own website has its team, collaborators, events and publications: guiltymindslab.com.

The research

Criminal law distinguishes kinds of guilty mind, or mens rea: intention, knowledge, recklessness and negligence. The lab studies how people ascribe these mental states and which biases distort the ascriptions. The work draws on theory of mind, moral psychology and the law.

The studies make three comparisons: lay jurors with professional judges, people without legal training with legal experts, and the criminal law of different countries, among them Switzerland, France, Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States. A further aim is to find out which measures reduce these biases, and what follows for criminal jurisprudence.

The team

The lab brings together philosophers, cognitive scientists and computer scientists working on moral and legal cognition and the ethics of AI. The current members and collaborators are listed on the lab’s team page.
