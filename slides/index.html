More about my work

I am Professor of Ethics of Artificial Intelligence at the University of Graz and direct the Guilty Minds Lab. My research asks how people assign responsibility, blame and trust, to one another and increasingly to machines, and what this means for how AI should be built, regulated and used.

Read more about my research, the NIHAI project on rules for conversations with AI, my publications and my CV.
